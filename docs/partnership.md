# Conditional legal-aid partnership approach

19 November 2026. One-page planning note; no outreach or agreement exists.

**Proposal:** explore a free, referral-first Sinhala procedural-information
navigator with the Legal Aid Commission and attorney/Sinhala/safeguarding
reviewers. Present original source text and citations, reviewed explanations
only, multiple candidates and conservative abstention. Ask a prospective partner
to validate scope, contact eligibility/access, regional routes, urgent alternatives,
professional review and a manageable review cadence. BASL's checked listing is
a general-office enquiry, not an agreed review programme.

**Evidence boundary:** the follow-up found the actual UNDP-published Legal Aid
Dialogue report and summary, both listed on 12 July 2026. The summary's physical
page 7 lists “launch legal aid hotline and digital intake system” under
“Immediate (within 12 months)”. See [primary-source evidence and hashes](partnership-verification.md).
This verifies a published reform priority, not a currently available service or
an LAi integration commitment. Current implementation, operator/channel details
and any agreement remain UNVERIFIED; no calendar launch deadline is inferred.
Earlier unsuccessful URL attempts remain recorded in the T8 log. No
email/telephone/message was sent, and no active referral record was changed.

**Offer and ask:** demonstrate a closed synthetic prototype and its NO-GO
ledger, then seek a small defined review pilot only after separate user approval
for outreach. Request official review/contact ownership and a verified service
description, not endorsements or exclusive representation. Agree on permitted
domains, stale-source handling, urgent safeguarding, copy/version review and
feedback consisting of non-personal aggregate issues. No real client facts in
engineering tickets or golden-set fixtures.

**Future intake handoff:** current UI only shows scoped published contacts;
the user initiates their own contact. A future partner-controlled connector would
require separate explicit consent/review, an agreed local endpoint/schema and
controller/processor terms. Start with a minimal envelope: schema/version,
reviewed route ID, preferred language, and user-approved short procedural topic.
Exclude raw scenarios, identity/contact/health/allegation fields by default.
Any contact-back mechanism needs a justified minimum, separate retention and
secure custody decision. Never send urgency/model scores or legal conclusions
as facts about a person. Do not persist this envelope or forward it automatically
in the current prototype.

**Acceptance:** partner validates receipt/eligibility, safety, privacy, refusal,
timeouts, duplicates, deletion, escalation responsibility and operating hours
using synthetic messages before a real pilot. The no-egress architecture means
external intake is not enabled today; any exception requires new architecture,
DPIA/counsel and launch review. No hidden network grant, invented hotline,
partnership promise, deadline or production endpoint is supplied by this note.
